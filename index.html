About Me

I am a freelance UI/UX Designer based in Nairobi. My approach to design is rooted in understanding. I don’t just focus on how something looks, but how it works, who it serves, and how it will evolve over time.

I consider the entire ecosystem — the user, the developer, the business, and the product itself. This allows me to create solutions that are not only visually appealing, but also practical, scalable, and efficient.

I’ve worked across different stages of the product lifecycle including UX research, wireframing, UI design, branding, front-end development, and system thinking.
